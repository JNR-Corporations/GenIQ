# 🚀 GenIq-jn

### JEE Main & JEE Advanced — Learning & Practice Platform

**GenIq-jn** is a structured JEE preparation platform designed to provide high-yield study material, topic-wise notes, formula sheets, visual explanations, practice tests, and revision resources for **JEE Main and JEE Advanced**.

The platform is developed by **Yogendra (Raj) Verma** and published under **JNR Corporations**.

---

## 🎯 What is GenIq-jn?

GenIq-jn focuses on making JEE preparation more structured and efficient by organizing study material into:

* 📚 Subject-wise resources
* 📖 Chapter-wise notes
* 🧩 Sub-topic breakdowns
* ⚡ High-yield formulas
* 🎯 JEE Main & Advanced concepts
* 📊 Graph-based explanations
* 📝 Practice questions
* 🧠 PYQ-oriented concepts
* 📄 PDF notes & handnotes
* 🏆 Advanced-level problem solving

The primary goal is to help students revise important concepts quickly while maintaining an organized learning experience.

```text
Subject
   ↓
Chapter
   ↓
Sub-topic
   ↓
Concept
   ↓
Formula
   ↓
Example
   ↓
PYQ / Practice
   ↓
Revision
```

This structure is designed to make large chapters easier to study and revise.
